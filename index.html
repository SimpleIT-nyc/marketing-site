How to reset your VPN client after an update
Knowledge Base · IT Operations · Published

If your VPN client shows a certificate error after the latest update, a local profile reset usually resolves it.

Steps

Quit the client completely, then clear its cached profile:

vpnclient --reset-profile --user $USER
Tip: you don't need to reinstall the client, since the reset only clears the local session cache.

Relaunch the client and sign in again with your usual credentials.
